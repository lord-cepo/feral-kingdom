# Synergies

General directions for designing cards and mechanics. These are design ideas;
specific card texts, balance values, and new interaction rules remain to be decided.
The current rules are in [GENERAL_RULES.md](GENERAL_RULES.md).

## Core themes

- **Bow:** enablers that upgrade Bow damage or range, and cards that benefit from
  wounding with Bow. Other cards can help archers find targets or follow up their
  wounds with movement, Stun, or another attack.
- **Fire:** benefits when Fire fizzles, and combined attacks triggered by specific
  conditions that build toward one large Fire attack. Failed lethal attempts can
  prepare a stronger attack later. A miss and contact that fails to defeat the
  target are separate possible payoff conditions.
- **Cannon:** the ideal enabler for "wounded by a friend" synergies. Cards can
  benefit from surviving friendly fire through bonuses, healing, movement, or
  resources. The approved 2026-10-28 ruling includes Cannon in shared Hit/Wound:
  contact is Hit, and positive applied life loss is Wound.
- **Reset:** enablers that Reset, and cards that benefit from restoring their
  printed state, especially cards with a powerful single-use action. Reset can
  also remove enemy upgrades or friendly drawbacks. ETB reuse belongs to Return
  and redeployment; Reset itself does not trigger ETB.
- **ETB (Deploy):** units that suppress ETB effects, paired with units whose bad
  ETB effects compensate for strong bodies or abilities. Other cards can reward
  deployment or use Return and redeployment to repeat beneficial ETB effects.
- **Soul:** cards that distribute or transfer Souls, make units with Soul effects
  interact, and provide different ways to earn and spend Souls. Some cards can
  reward holding Souls while others reward spending them.
- **Bomb:** ways to displace bombs, produce new bombs, light them, and defuse them.
  Synergies can reward placement, merging, ignition, surviving explosions, or
  recovering value from defused bombs.
- **Shield:** units that provide Shield and units that gain bonuses near friends
  with high modifiers. Other directions include transferring protection, spending
  temporary protection as a resource, and benefiting when protection is consumed.
  Mshield can support similar themes around magical attacks.
- **Sacrifice:** a future keyword that defeats a friend on the board in exchange
  for bonuses. It works with extra deployment allowance (+deploy), Revive, death
  effects, and cards that benefit from other friends being sacrificed.
- **Stun:** ways to increase Stun and interact with its counters. Cards can benefit
  while stunned, reward recovery, or exchange Stun for another resource or bonus.
  This creates a choice between delaying a unit's actions and gaining value now.
- **Pull/Push:** ways to gain time by attacking and then displacing a participant
  to dodge the counterattack. Pull can also start Bashes. Variable payments can
  support actions such as "spend X Souls to Push N hexes," with distance or other
  parameters depending on the payment. Forced movement also sets up Cannon lines
  and bomb blasts.

## Additional themes

- **Regional control:** cards that help take or hold regions, and cards that gain
  bonuses from fighting in controlled or contested regions. Movement, healing,
  and wounds can change control without defeating a unit.
- **Wounds and healing:** units that become stronger while wounded, ways to pay
  life for benefits, and cards that reward restoring life. Healing creates a
  choice between safety, regional strength, and keeping wounded bonuses.
- **Formation:** bonuses for adjacency, straight lines, surrounding an enemy, or
  being isolated. Different formations can support archers, Cannon users,
  defensive groups, or lone attackers.
- **Return and reserves:** ways to Return friends, rewards for friendly Returns,
  and benefits from keeping cards undeployed. These connect Soul generation,
  redeployment, ETB effects, and deployment allowance.
- **Activity and recovery:** units that support friends while inactive, cards that
  reward activation or Stun recovery, and bonuses for resting instead of acting.
  Acting can mean giving up a useful support effect until the unit rests again.
- **Delayed attacks and dodges:** cards that benefit when an enemy moves to escape
  a pending attack, and cards that help friends dodge. Even a missed attack can
  create value by forcing movement or giving up a useful position.
- **Finishing attacks:** rewards for defeating enemies, combining several attacks
  against one target, or committing more attack power than necessary. These can
  connect Bow wounds to Fire finishers and reward preparing one large attack.
- **Death locations:** benefits around the hex where a unit died, such as gaining
  Souls, placing bombs, or rewarding movement toward that location. These connect
  sacrifice and death effects with positioning.
